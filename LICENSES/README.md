# Licenses and provenance

The `console` submodule is the product repository, not a relicensed copy.
Its dependencies keep their own licenses. Wally-derived code and patches retain
their existing notices; see [Wally.txt](Wally.txt). RasterIX-derived code retains
its existing notices; see [RasterIX.txt](RasterIX.txt). Some copied files state
additional upstream licenses in their headers. Those notices remain applicable.
The archived memory tester retains its
[GNU GPL version 2 license](../experiments/board-bringup-20260909/source/memtester-4.7.1/COPYING).

Research prose and newly written laboratory/publication tools are published
here without applying a blanket software license to the entire collection.
Public visibility alone does not grant additional reuse rights. A repository-wide
license for original research materials has not been chosen yet.

Personal attribution belonging to the research author is replaced in public
copies at their request. Upstream third-party authorship is retained. Figures
in the thesis are explanatory research diagrams; cited external books and
websites are references, not included copies of those publications.
