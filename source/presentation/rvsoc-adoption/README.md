# RVSOC textbook adoption evidence

`records.json` records public textbook designations and instructor-reported
course use, including historical use of prepublication drafts. Its three
institutions are examples, not a complete inventory of current adopters.

The active builder reads this data through
[`rvsoc-adoption.mjs`](../board-evolution/rvsoc-adoption.mjs) and renders an
editable table on hidden slide 14. The main eleven slides and their
355-second narration allocation remain unchanged.

Use the [published source register](../../../docs/presentation/RVSOC教科書採用の出典.md)
for course periods, source locations and reference-only or unverified cases.
Do not count author affiliations, library holdings, bookstore entries or
matching course titles as textbook adoption. Keep draft use distinct from
use of a published edition and dates distinct from the last checked date.
Source pages are linked rather than redistributed in full.
